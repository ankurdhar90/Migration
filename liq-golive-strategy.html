LIQ migration · go-live strategy for SteerCo

Three states, each one reversible

Tier-1 moves to Snowflake in two controlled steps instead of one. The in-and-out step swaps only the data source and keeps SSIS. Go-live then removes SSIS and makes the Snowflake tables the tier-1 tables. Each step is proven on its own and can be rolled back to the one before.

  • Snowflake route
  • Data gate
  • X stands for every Transactional, Reference and Config table

The path, with a gate and a way back at every step

Each move forward needs a go/no-go decision. Each state can roll back to the one before, and the in-and-out step keeps the first rollback down to an alias flip.

Go-live strategies considered

SteerCo asked for every strategy we could use. These are the realistic ones, rated by judgement against the criteria SteerCo cares about. Green is better for every column.

Phased by subject area carries a hidden risk: Deposits and Loans tables that join on surrogate keys could sit in two key spaces at once. If you phase, group tables by their join dependencies, not by business name.

Making go-live as safe as the cutover

A plain rename makes go-live the riskiest step: rollback means reversing the rename and rerunning every live date. Blue/green keeps the legacy table loaded during hypercare, so rollback is one synonym change with nothing to rerun.

What each gate needs

These are what gives SteerCo the confidence. Agree them before the first rehearsal.

G1

Enter in and out

  • Shadow run clean at all 4 levels, including a month-end
  • Catalog check passed, and the dedicated alias is in place
  • Key approach decided and tested across the cutover date
  • Alias flip and rollback rehearsed and timed
  • Data gate and Control-M changes live in pre-prod
G2

Enter go-live

  • In and out stable for an agreed run, including a month-end
  • Tier-1 history backfilled and reconciled
  • Grants, FKs, views and indexes inventoried and scripted
  • Publish proc and table swap rehearsed, both forward and back
  • Tier-2 confirmed safe on synonyms, if blue/green
G3

Decommission

  • Clean hypercare period agreed and met
  • Business sign-off on the liquidity reports
  • No open breaks above tolerance
  • Path marker shows Snowflake for every date
  • CDM consumers confirmed before legacy is switched off

Confirmed and assumed

Confirmedby you

  • Three states: today, in and out (the alias cutover), then go-live.
  • In and out: the alias points to thin LIQ procs that read SnowIn_*, and SSIS still loads tier-1.
  • Go-live: no SSIS, and SnowIn tables become the tier-1 tables tier-2 reads.
  • Tier-1 keeps history. Downstream is unchanged.
  • SteerCo wants every go-live strategy considered, with low risk as the goal.

Assumedfor discussion

  • Blue/green is offered as a lower-risk way to do the go-live swap.
  • The go-live swap happens once, and daily loads then go through land, gate and publish.
  • The legacy CDM chain stays fed until G3.
  • The strategy ratings are judgement, to be checked with the team.
